Comprehensive Development Plan 2045

Bhubaneswar, Odisha

DemographicsHousehold surveyProjection

Demographic and socio-economic analysis using GIS and statistical projection. Arithmetic, incremental and ratio methods were compared, and the incremental method suited the survey results best. The work includes ward-wise density maps, income inequality (Gini 0.44), and housing-scheme beneficiary patterns.
