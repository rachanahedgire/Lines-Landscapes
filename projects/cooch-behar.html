Greenfield Master Plan 2041

Cooch Behar, West Bengal

Master planIndustrial cityGIS

A new industrial city planned around the Mega Food Park model. Sites were selected for connectivity to roads, rail and the airport, distance from heritage and the river, and room for independent expansion of both city and industry. The plan covers land use, transport hierarchy and freight corridor, an industrial development zone, zonal development control, and a built-form simulation.
