Action Area Planning

Ward 28, Kharagpur, West Bengal

PMAYLiDARAffordable housing

In-situ redevelopment of a slum pocket under PMAY. The site was scanned with a Leica BLK360, processed in Cyclone REGISTER 360 and Autodesk Recap, then modelled in Revit. The proposal has two stilt+4 towers, upgraded roads and water supply, and a full cost estimate.
