Managing Urban Flooding

Mumbai, Maharashtra

Risk assessmentSpatial analysisResilience

A systematic look at why Mumbai floods. About 35% of residents live within 250 m of flood hotspots, and 73% of industrial and commercial establishments lie within 500 m. Mapping of drainage, hospitals, shelters and jobs led to proposals: SuDS, sponge parks, watershed management, natural drain restoration, a drainage capacity target of 50 mm per hour, and non-structural measures on zoning, regulation and awareness.

Focus
Systemic causes of flooding
